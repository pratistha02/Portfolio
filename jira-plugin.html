The problem

What was broken

The plugin worked. Administrators could import users, schedule cleanup rules and read audit logs. The problem was how those tasks arrived: as single dense screens where meaning was carried almost entirely by background colour, and where the setting that deactivates real accounts sat at the same visual weight as the notification preference beside it.

An independent redesign exploration grounded in heuristic evaluation, comparative analysis, simulated administrator tasks and established interaction patterns.
